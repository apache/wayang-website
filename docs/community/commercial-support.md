Companies offering commercial support, products, or services based on or around Apache Wayang are listed below.

The Apache Software Foundation and the Apache Wayang Project Management Committee (PMC) do not endorse or recommend any provider, product, or service listed on this page. Please contact providers directly to discuss their offerings and terms.

## Providers

### Scalytics

[Scalytics](https://www.scalytics.io/apache-wayang) offers commercial support and integration services for Apache Wayang through a Wayang add-on to its Lascaris platform. Its offering includes tested releases, security maintenance, support with defined service-level agreements, and deployment in customer environments, including on-premise and air-gapped installations. Integration and onboarding services include migrating existing Spark and Flink jobs and tuning Wayang's cost model to the customer's cluster and data.

## Add or update a listing

If your company offers commercial support, products, or services based on or around Apache Wayang, contact [dev@wayang.apache.org](mailto:dev@wayang.apache.org) to request a listing. Include your company name, website, and a short factual description of your Wayang offering.

Existing providers can use the same mailing list to request updates or removal. Listings are reviewed by the Apache Wayang community and are kept in alphabetical order.

Descriptions should be factual and informational. Please keep promotional claims and comparisons with other providers on your own website.

## Community support

You can also ask questions and discuss Apache Wayang on the public mailing list. See [Mailing Lists](./mailinglists.md) for subscription details and archives. Community support is provided by volunteers.
